Hey Surgi — Speech Recognition & ROS 2 Robot Control

A proof-of-concept human–machine interaction system connecting live speech recognition with command parsing and ROS 2 client–service communication.

Objective

Evaluate speech-recognition models for a command-driven robotics workflow and develop a prototype capable of translating recognized speech into ROS 2 command requests.

My documented contributions

  • Benchmarked eight automatic speech recognition models using 45 audio samples.
  • Compared recognition quality and transcription latency; Faster-Whisper-Distil-Medium.en achieved a reported similarity score of 0.86 and an average transcription time of 0.16 seconds in the project evaluation.
  • Implemented microphone input, speech-to-command matching, negation handling, and ROS 2 client–service communication.
  • Tested commands using personal recordings and documented performance and limitations.

Scope

This was a proof-of-concept prototype, not a clinically validated system or deployed medical device. Reported benchmark figures depend on the project's dataset, hardware and evaluation method.
